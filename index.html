OUR DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

S A H H A is an architectural & interiors design firm headed by Ar. Pavan Kumar & Ar. Naveen B. G, registered architects from Council of Architecture & Bangalore Mahanagara Palike.

We strive for an architecture/interiors that are thoughtful and purposeful. We create functional solutions that are emotive and evocative; spirited, graceful and imbued with whimsy, wit and optimism.

Our design celebrates the simplicity of elemental materials, the truth of nature, our bodies’ movement through spaces, and the delight of the sun’s animating light. Our work is dynamic, imaginative, environmentally sustainable, and technologically advanced, committed to empowering our clients while addressing local contexts and regional programs.

"Our design strength lies in fitting buildings to the land and paying special attention to the craft of assembling materials. We seek to celebrate the extraordinary within the ordinary."

Ar. Pavan Kumar & Ar. Naveen B. G

Registered Architects & Founders
